Before buying

Ask these four questions

  • Are you located in Lapu-Lapu or Cebu City?
  • How many inches / what are the dimensions?
  • Do the wheels, handle, and zippers work?
  • Can I inspect and pick up locally?

Safer pickup tips

  • Meet in a public place, ideally daytime.
  • Check for broken wheels, cracked shells, and stuck zippers.
  • Compare transport costs before choosing a distant seller.
  • Avoid sending deposits to unverified sellers.

Alternative for a short move

If a good suitcase is hard to find, a sturdy reusable moving bag can be enough for the trip to Cebu City. No need to rush into an overpriced suitcase.
